Cards

Card data from TCGdex, with pokemontcg.io as an English backup. Prices: TCGplayer (English cards) and Cardmarket (Japanese cards, and any card TCGplayer has no price for). Optional PriceCharting prices with your own API key. Exchange rates: open.er-api.com.

Prices are for one copy of each card, ungraded. The sources publish new prices about once a day; this page checks for them on the refresh timer.

Your collection is saved in this browser. Use Export now and then to keep a backup file, or to move it to another device.

Not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, TCGplayer or PriceCharting.
